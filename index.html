I lead pretraining team at xAI. I'm the pretraining lead of Grok-4.2/4.1/4-fast and Grok 2/3/4-mini, pretraining lead for Grok-Voice on X and Tesla, and core pretraining recipe contributor to Grok 2/3/4.
Before joining xAI, I worked as a Research Scientist at OpenAI. I'm a core contributor to GPT-4o, and the inventor of GPT4-Turbo long-context algorithm, and a primary contributor to DALL-E 3, and the first contributor to OpenAI Embedding.
